Common Questions

Can I upgrade my plan later? expand_more

Yes, you can upgrade your membership at any time through the NexusDesk app. The difference in price will be pro-rated for the current billing cycle.

What are meeting room credits? expand_more

1 credit is equal to 1 hour of use for a small meeting room (up to 4 people). Larger rooms may require 2 or 3 credits per hour. Credits reset monthly.
